AUTOMOTIVE DUE DILIGENCE

More Than an Inspection.

Automotive Due Diligence is a structured process for gathering, documenting, and reconciling evidence about a vehicle before you make an expensive decision.

Onyx MotorWorks examines multiple evidence sources rather than relying on a single inspection point. The goal is not to eliminate uncertainty. It is to systematically increase the amount and quality of evidence available before you decide.

THE EVIDENCE

Multiple Sources.
One Better-Informed Decision.

Automotive Due Diligence examines evidence across the vehicle and considers how individual findings relate to one another.

01

Vehicle Identity & Documentation

VIN, configuration, available records, and vehicle-specific information.

02

Exterior & Finish Evidence

Body condition, coating measurements, repair and refinish indicators, glass, lighting, and exterior observations.

03

Mechanical & Electronic Evidence

Accessible mechanical systems, operating observations, diagnostic information, and electronic evidence.

04

Interior & Equipment Evidence

Interior condition, controls, equipment, features, and accessible vehicle systems.

05

Underbody & Structural Evidence

Visible underbody and structural areas, corrosion, damage indicators, leaks, repairs, and other observations accessible without disassembly or lifting.

06

Evidence Reconciliation

Findings are considered together to identify consistencies, discrepancies, and areas requiring additional attention.

01

Request

Submit the vehicle, transaction, and seller information needed for OM to review the requested service.

02

Review & Coordination

OM reviews the request, confirms service applicability, and coordinates inspection access with the seller or dealer.

03

Terms & Intake

Once the request is approved, review the service scope, limitations, applicable terms, and final inspection details before the appointment.

04

Inspection

The vehicle is examined as-present using the applicable OM Automotive Due Diligence process and evidence standards.

05

Reconciliation

Findings from multiple evidence sources are considered together to identify consistencies, discrepancies, and areas requiring additional attention.

06

Report

Documented findings, supporting evidence, and applicable limitations are organized into the final due diligence report.
