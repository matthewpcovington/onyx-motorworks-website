AUTOMOTIVE DUE DILIGENCE

More Than an Inspection.

Automotive Due Diligence is a structured process for gathering, documenting, and reconciling evidence about a vehicle before you make an expensive decision.

Onyx MotorWorks examines multiple evidence sources rather than relying on a single inspection point. The goal is not to eliminate uncertainty. It is to systematically increase the amount and quality of evidence available before you decide.

THE EVIDENCE

Multiple Sources.

Automotive Due Diligence examines evidence across the vehicle and considers how individual findings relate to one another.

WHAT WE EXAMINE

Evidence collected across the vehicle.
Considered as a whole.

Automotive Due Diligence brings multiple forms of observable and documented evidence into a single structured examination.

01

Vehicle Identity & Records

Vehicle identifiers, publicly available records, seller-provided information, and other available documentation are reviewed and compared for consistency.

DOCUMENT · COMPARE
02

Exterior Condition

Body panels, exterior components, glass, lighting, wheels and tires, visible damage, wear, and other observable exterior conditions are documented.

OBSERVE · DOCUMENT

Finish & Repair Evidence

Surface condition, coating measurements, visible repair or refinish indicators, panel relationships, and other observable evidence are documented and compared.

MEASURE · COMPARE
04

Interior & Controls

Interior condition, equipment, controls, displays, features, and observable signs of wear, damage, modification, or abnormality are examined.

OBSERVE · DOCUMENT
05

Systems & Operational Observations

Accessible vehicle systems, functions, and operating characteristics are observed within the applicable ADD procedure and the conditions available at the time of inspection.

OPERATE · OBSERVE
06
ANALYSIS

Evidence Reconciliation

Records, measurements, photographs, observations, and other collected evidence are considered together rather than treating individual findings in isolation.

RECORDS + MEASUREMENTS + PHOTOGRAPHS + OBSERVATIONS
↓ DOCUMENTED FINDINGS
AS-PRESENT NON-DISASSEMBLY GROUND-LEVEL EVIDENCE-BASED

OM Automotive Due Diligence examines the vehicle in the condition and environment available at the time of inspection. Components are not removed or disassembled, and launch inspections are conducted from ground level. Findings describe the evidence available during the inspection and do not establish conditions that cannot be observed or reasonably evaluated within the service scope.

08   SERVICE BOUNDARIES

What the examination does.
And what it does not.

Automotive Due Diligence increases the amount and quality of evidence available before an expensive vehicle decision while remaining clear about what that evidence can establish.

WHAT IT DOES
01

Documents observable condition

Records visible and accessible vehicle conditions present at the time of examination.

02

Collects supporting evidence

Uses records, measurements, photographs, and observations to increase the evidence available for review.

03

Reconciles findings

Considers individual observations together to identify where available evidence supports, conflicts, or remains inconclusive.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
01

Disassemble the vehicle

Components are not removed or disassembled during the examination.

02

Establish unobservable conditions

Conditions outside the accessible service scope are not represented as observed or verified.

03

Guarantee future condition

Findings document evidence available at the time of examination and do not guarantee future performance, reliability, or condition.
